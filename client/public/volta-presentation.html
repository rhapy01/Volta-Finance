The project

Business finance, built around BCH.

Volta Finance helps a business create invoices, receive BCH or CashTokens, organize staff payroll, and keep transaction-backed financial records in one workspace. Compatible BCH wallets connect through WizardConnect when someone is ready to approve a payment.

PAYMENT ASSETS
BCHPUSDMUSD

Users review a transaction in their wallet before signing. Volta verifies the resulting payment before recording it as settled.

The story

Business money work is split across too many steps.

A team may invoice in one place, arrange payroll in another, and still have to check the blockchain to know what actually settled. Volta Finance brings these tasks into one BCH-focused workspace and ties payment records to verified transaction results, while the wallet owner keeps control of signing.
